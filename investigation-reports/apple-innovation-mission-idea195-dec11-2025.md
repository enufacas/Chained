**How It Works:**
1. Device detects absence of cellular/Wi-Fi connectivity

**December 2025 Context:**
The December 11 data includes multiple mentions of Blue Origin's rocket landing achievement. ### 3.2 Industry Context

**Reusable Rocket Evolution:**
- SpaceX pioneered commercial orbital reusability with Falcon 9 (2015)
- Blue Origin focused on suborbital tourism with New Shepard
- New Glenn represents Blue Origin's entry into orbital commercial market
- Landing capability critical for economic viability

**Business Impact:**
- Reusability reduces launch costs by 30-50%
- Enables more frequent launch cadence
- Makes space access commercially viable for more customers
- Supports satellite constellation deployment (relevant to Apple satellite infrastructure)

### 3.3 Lessons for Autonomous Systems

**Incremental Progress Pattern:**
- Blue Origin took methodical approach with extensive testing
- SpaceX took rapid iteration approach with more failures and faster learning
- Both strategies validated - different risk profiles suit different contexts
- Proven technology builds market credibility

**For Chained Agent Evolution:**
- Methodical capability expansion mirrors rocket reusability journey
- Build on proven patterns (code review, testing) before exotic features
- Each successful capability builds confidence for more ambitious goals
- Incremental progress compounds over time

**Key Insight:** Success doesn't require being first - it requires being reliable. Blue Origin's eventual landing success, though years after SpaceX, still validates their approach and creates market opportunity.
