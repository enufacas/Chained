### 🔑 Most Valuable Insight

**The Critical-to-Routine Expansion Pattern:**

Technologies consistently follow a pattern of starting with critical/emergency use cases, proving reliability, then expanding to routine/everyday applications:
- **Apple Satellite:** Emergency SOS → general messaging
- **GPS:** Military/emergency → consumer navigation
- **Agent Evolution:** Bug fixes/security patches → feature development/refactoring

**Application to Chained:** Start agents with high-stakes workflows where automation provides clear value and risk is manageable. As reliability is proven, expand to routine development tasks. This builds trust incrementally rather than attempting everything at once.

This pattern appears across multiple innovation categories in this mission, making it the most transferable and actionable insight.
