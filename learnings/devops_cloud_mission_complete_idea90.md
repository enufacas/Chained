## Stories Analyzed

### 1. Checkout.com Ransomware Incident (HN Score: 425)

**Key Points:**
- Legacy cloud storage system compromised
- Company refused ransom, donated to security research
- Full transparency in communication
- Enhanced reputation through ethical response

**Lesson:** Every undecommissioned system is a potential attack vector. Formal retirement protocols critical.

### 2. MongoDB 90% Cost Reduction (HN Score: 136)

**Key Points:**
- Migration from MongoDB Atlas (AWS) to Hetzner
- $3,000/month → $300/month (90% reduction)
- Data transfer fees were 33% of original costs
- Self-management trade-offs accepted for savings

**Lesson:** European cloud providers competitive for predictable workloads with mature teams.
